## Platform

web

## Users

The primary visitors are founders, executives, and senior product or technology leaders evaluating Pranshul Chandhok for strategic advisory, founder collaboration, difficult 0-to-1 product work, or selective VP/CTO-level opportunities. Secondary visitors include journalists, event organizers, technical peers, and potential collaborators looking for credible expertise in generative AI, agentic systems, real-time 3D, XR, and spatial computing.

Visitors usually arrive from LinkedIn, X, GitHub, a referral, or a branded search. They need to understand quickly what Pranshul builds, what makes his experience unusual, what evidence supports it, and how to start a relevant conversation.

## Product Purpose

2600th.com is Pranshul Chandhok's personal portfolio, professional presence, and experimental lab. It should turn public work, career depth, technical judgment, and current experiments into high-quality inbound conversations without reading like a job-seeker portfolio or a development agency.

Success means that qualified visitors can identify Pranshul as a hands-on product and engineering operator, find relevant proof, remember the compound AI-plus-real-time-3D advantage, and confidently initiate a conversation about a difficult product problem.

## Positioning

Pranshul repeatedly turns emerging AI and spatial technologies into products that survive production. His differentiator is the combination of 15-plus years in real-time 3D, games, XR, robotics, and performance engineering with current 0-to-1 leadership in generative AI, multimodal systems, agentic workflows, and consumer product development.

## Operating Context

The work spans product strategy, technical architecture, applied AI, evaluation and reliability, real-time rendering, game engines, WebGPU and Three.js, XR, design technology, interior and real-estate workflows, cross-functional leadership, and rapid public experimentation. Evidence appears across professional case studies, open-source repositories, public posts, videos, talks, recommendations, patents, awards, and an older GreyKernel project archive.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The first release may use only public evidence, publicly shared screenshots and videos, open-source material, and explicitly approved claims.
- Employer, client, defense, or proprietary material must not expose unpublished metrics, customer information, internal architecture, confidential workflows, or unapproved media.
- Individual case studies may later be enhanced with employer-approved screenshots, diagrams, videos, or metrics.
- The site may use JavaScript animation, UI, audio, music, WebGL, canvas, or other libraries when they materially improve the experience and remain performant, accessible, responsive, and respectful of reduced-motion and user audio preferences.
- Important content, navigation, calls to action, and evidence must remain semantic and crawlable rather than existing only in canvas or animation.
- The experience must work across desktop and mobile and degrade gracefully when advanced rendering or audio is unavailable.

## Product Principles

1. **Proof before posture.** Demonstrate shipped systems, experiments, decisions, and outcomes instead of relying on broad capability claims.
2. **Human first, alter ego second.** Make Pranshul immediately legible while letting 2600th carry experimentation, surprise, and memorable interaction.
3. **Production is the differentiator.** Show the path from emerging technology to reliable product, not only attractive demos.
4. **Depth without a resume dump.** Use selected case studies and an explorable archive to reveal range progressively.
5. **Playfulness earns attention.** Motion, sound, 3D, and interaction should express the work and reward curiosity without slowing comprehension or contact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Provide keyboard access, visible focus, semantic landmarks, sufficient contrast in both themes, reduced-motion alternatives, captions or transcripts for meaningful video, descriptive text for imagery, and explicit opt-in audio with persistent mute controls. No essential information may depend on color, motion, sound, hover, or a high-performance GPU.
