I made a thirty-second science-fiction fight with MiniMax H3 running locally on an RTX 5090 and Seedance 2.5.

The meaningful leap was not simply prettier frames. It was control. As production becomes less of a gatekeeper, story, taste, direction, and the agency to finish become the moat.
