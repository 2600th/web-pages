After watching *Operation Safed Sagar*, I wanted to see whether the feeling of a high-altitude reconnaissance flight could become a small browser experience.

A few days later, the experiment was running at a URL: no install and no game-engine download. The result is fictional, respectful in intent, and deliberately transparent about its historical distance.
