With IRA VR, we had to think about the whole class. A learner might be inside a virtual environment, but the teacher still needed to choose the material, coordinate the session, and understand what happened afterwards.

I worked on both sides of that experience. The virtual content was the most visible part; the controls and reporting helped make it something a teacher could use as part of a lesson.
