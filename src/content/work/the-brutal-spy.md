Released for iPhone and iPad in January 2012, The Brutal Spy was a top-down action-adventure and stealth shooter. Players controlled Razor, a field operative, through close-quarters combat and encounters with armed enemies using touchscreen controls.

I worked on The Brutal Spy as a game programmer. It was one of my early experiences of developing a game for release on mobile hardware. The small screen and touchscreen controls made responsiveness especially important, while the action had to remain readable during combat.
