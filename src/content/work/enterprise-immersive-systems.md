For JPMorgan Chase, the surviving engagement record documents a direct 2018 scope for live-action VR/360 production in Boston, including capture and post-production. It belongs here as enterprise production work—not as a claim that GreyKernel built an unrelated banking platform.

Anglian Water commissioned a multi-module training application spanning the main client/server and demonstration UI, 360-still vehicle inspection, and safe-driving and safe-digging modules. Voxel Worlds VR reached Apple's TestFlight and App Review pipeline as version 1.8. The wider record spans medical and industrial training, maritime simulation, accessibility work with Sight Savers, analytics and visualization with TheMathCompany, hospitality, production-facility showcases, and interactive real estate. Myntra, Swissotel, and United Ocean are named client contexts in the archive; their exact public scope is not expanded beyond the evidence available.

Across these engagements, Pranshul's role connected product definition, real-time 3D and XR architecture, engineering delivery, domain constraints, and the operating workflows required to make an immersive system useful beyond a demonstration.
