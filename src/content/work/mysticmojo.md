MysticMojo was our game studio within GreyKernel. I helped set it up and led technology while the team developed mobile and AR titles. The projects gave us room to work on our own characters as well as familiar ones from licensed properties.

In Chhota Bheem Jungle Rescue, the player flies across Dholakpur, collects water, and uses it to put out jungle fires. The controls were kept simple for younger players. Between the alpha handoff and release, the work included balancing the game, finishing sound, making dialogue skippable, and improving performance on older phones.
