## Additional production constraints

- [Anthropic's Claude Code introduction](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-3-7-sonnet), checked 3 September: repository search and reading, file edits, running tests and debugging support the narrow coding-agent capability statement. The source introduced a limited research preview; it is not cited as a current availability guide or proof of productivity gains. The essay's second-edit test is the owner's verification criterion, not a measured benchmark or a claim that agents replace a team.
- [Muse introduction, 19 February 2025](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/introducing-muse-our-first-generative-ai-model-designed-for-gameplay-ideation/): Bleeding Edge training domain, 300×180 output, consistency/diversity/persistency, open weights and concept demonstrator. Do not present as a shipping game engine or compare its conditions directly with Genie.
- [Steamworks Content Survey](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/contentsurvey), checked 3 September: player-consumed pre-generated content versus live-generated content; the latter additionally requires describing guardrails against illegal content. Disclosure does not guarantee acceptance.
- [US Copyright Office initiative](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/) and [Part 2, January 2025, executive summary pp. ii–iii](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/Copyright-and-Artificial-Intelligence-Part-2-Copyrightability-Report.pdf): human expression and control; prompt-only input insufficient under its analysis of then-current technology; human selection, arrangement or modification assessed case by case. US-specific publishing consideration, not legal advice or a worldwide rule.
- [PUBG publisher patch 42.1, 16 June 2026](https://www.pubg.com/en/news/10179?category=patch_notes): additional limits including outdated knowledge, no reconnect, and advice to lower graphics when responses lag. Useful check against treating vendor architecture as measured latency.

## First-person source boundary

The existing published Kinema, Safed Sagar and Ocean cases and the August Field Note support the owner's account of AI-assisted iteration followed by direct work on editor controls, input, loading, rendering and compatibility. Their public repositories are linked in the essay. No new tests or productivity measurements are attributed to the owner. Ocean's pinned [performance record](https://github.com/2600th/web-ocean-3d/blob/6496c77/docs/PERFORMANCE.md) compares asset budgets, not frame rate or total process memory.
