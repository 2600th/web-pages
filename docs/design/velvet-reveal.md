# Velvet Reveal

Mode: Experience for Home/Work/Lab; Read for Notes and case-study prose.

Preserve the velvet-black canvas, warm Mona Sans lettering, the 2600th character,
cobalt geometry and restrained gold interaction details. This is an incremental
refinement, with the Orbital hero and image-led gallery approved for integration on 6 September 2026, not a replacement visual world.

The hero is the one authored motion moment: cobalt orbital arcs, sparse
particles and a restrained moving gold detail. Mobile geometry follows the portrait rather than viewport-only coordinates. Geometry remains visible
when motion is off. Fine-pointer halo is bounded and absent from prose. Keep
native cursor and controls. No scroll-jacking, autoplay sound or hidden reading
content. Stop loops when offscreen or hidden; use static presentation for touch
and reduced-motion devices. A persistent Motion preference is available on desktop.

Mobile puts orientation, actions and actual work before decorative repetition.
Keep the character but shorten the opening. Interior title planes must not reserve
desktop height on a phone. Long-form content stays left aligned and navigable.

Work is an image-led editorial gallery, not a decorative collage followed by a list.
Blocks leads the selected view with Designesto and IRA VR beside it. Every project
remains available once; filtering and chronological order use uniform cards.
Project openings group their title, summary, metadata and image before bounded
reading content. Native contents navigation supports longer projects.

Retain authentic original assets. Approved enhanced covers can remain, with
reader-facing editorial context on standalone figures. Never manufacture private
metrics, source footage, benchmark comparisons or employer product screenshots.
